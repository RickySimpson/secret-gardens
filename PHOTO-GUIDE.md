# Secret Gardens Salon — Future Photography Guide

The redesign intentionally uses only the salon photography already in the repository. A future, on-location shoot could add range and specificity without changing the editorial direction.

## Recommended photographs

1. **Exterior arrival — 2400 × 1600 px (3:2, landscape).** Photograph the salon entrance and its Solvang context in soft morning or late-afternoon light. Keep signage readable and leave negative space for optional editorial copy.
2. **Family portrait — 1800 × 2400 px (3:4, portrait).** A relaxed portrait of the family members who operate the salon, made inside the space with natural window light. Avoid a formal staff-lineup composition.
3. **Craft details — 2000 × 2500 px (4:5, portrait), 4–6 images.** Hands at work, color mixing, precise cutting, manicure details, folded towels, and the tactile tools of the salon. Obtain model releases for recognizable guests.
4. **Interior atmosphere — 2400 × 1600 px (3:2, landscape), 2–3 images.** Wide and medium views showing the salon at its calmest. Preserve warm practical lighting and the room's true color; avoid ultra-wide distortion.
5. **Solvang sense of place — 2400 × 1600 px (3:2, landscape).** A restrained architectural detail or street approach near Alisal Road that connects the salon to the village without becoming a tourism image.
6. **Botanical still life — 1800 × 2400 px (3:4, portrait).** A real arrangement made in the salon using local greenery, paired with salon objects. Use this sparingly as an interlude image.

## Art direction and delivery

- Favor quiet, candid moments, natural skin texture, warm California light, earthy greens, cream, wood, and muted rose.
- Avoid stock-style smiles to camera, ring-light reflections, heavy presets, artificial foliage, visible third-party product branding, and trend-led props.
- Deliver high-resolution originals plus web-ready AVIF and WebP exports at 2400, 1600, 1200, and 800 px widths.
- Keep focal subjects away from extreme edges so images can crop safely at 16:9, 4:5, and 3:4.
- Record accurate alt-text notes, photographer credit requirements, usage rights, and signed releases with the final delivery.
