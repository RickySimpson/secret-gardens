A place with roots

A garden
tended by family.

Secret Gardens is more than a salon — it's a family legacy rooted in care, craft, and community.

Nestled in the charming Danish village of Solvang, our doors have always been open with warmth and a personal touch that only family can offer.

Our grandmother brought the vision. Our mother built upon it. Together, they have cultivated a space where every guest is treated like a dear friend — where you can sit down, breathe, and leave feeling truly cared for.
